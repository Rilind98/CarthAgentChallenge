# arbeitblatt — layout profile

- **doc_type per sheet**: Sheet1 is an Arbeitsversion/workpaper (auditor's tangible-assets audit sheet: account lead schedule, reconciliations to the Anlagenspiegel, significant additions/disposals, conclusions).
- **sheets**: 1 tab "Sheet1". The used range is A1:Q866, but the content ends at about row 375. No hidden sheets.
- **export origin guess**: hand-made Excel workpaper from an audit firm template, with pasted screenshots.
- **years/periods**: A4 holds the title "Prüfung des Jahresabschluss zum dd.mm.yyyy" (bold). Lead-schedule headings: G9 is text "<dd.mm.yyyy> (vor Prüfung)", H9 is a **real date** (prior year end), K9 is text "<yyyy> (nach Prüfung)". Dates also appear inside labels ("Buchwert per dd.mm.yyyy gem. …").
- **header block above the table**: row 1 is a coloured band A1:K1 (#00B0F0) with the workpaper index in L1 (bold, medium border, pattern "XX-X999/99"). Row 4 is the title. Rows 5–6 hold the sign-off block: "geprüft von" <preparer> "am" <date>, and "abgenommen von" <reviewer> "am" <reference>. L7 holds "Referenz" (bold). The lead schedule starts in row 9.
- **columns** (lead schedule, 1 heading row): A section label | B account no. | C account name | (D–F empty) | G current year before audit | H prior year | I Veränderung (`=G-H`) | (J empty) | K current year after audit (`=G` or a link to a reconciliation cell) | L remark (bold, e.g. "umgegliedert in …"). Column N holds the cross-reference to other workpapers (pattern "XX-X999.99") throughout the sheet.
- **row levels**: 1 level of account rows. Cost accounts and accumulated-depreciation accounts are **separate rows** ("Kum.Abschreibg. …", negative). Groups are shown only by the **fill colour of column G** (3 pastel theme colours: buildings / machinery / other equipment). There are no group subtotals.
- **subtotals/totals**: one bold `=SUM` total row under the schedule. The SUM ranges differ between columns (one column stops one row earlier). The reconciliation sections use small 2–3 line blocks: "… gem. Bilanz" / "… gem. Anlagespiegel" / "Abweichung" (`=a-b`, bold), with "i. O." when the result is 0. Sub-tables end in a bold "Summe" row.
- **number storage**: numbers as numbers, "#,##0.00". Credit (accumulated depreciation) accounts are negative. One disposal sub-table uses the accounting format with parentheses `_(* #,##0.00_);_(* \(#,##0.00\);…`. Some cells hold `=a+b` constants. The useful-life column uses "#,##0". Unit is EUR, implied.
- **dates / useful life / method codes**: the additions/intangibles sub-tables hold "Anschaffung" as a real date (short format), "ND in Monaten" as an integer, and "in Jahren" as `=H/12`. There are no method codes.
- **asset id pattern**: "Inventar-Nr" is 6 digits stored as **text**, leading zeros kept. Accounts in the lead schedule are integers of 3 or 5 digits (mixed).
- **arithmetic**: Veränderung = current − prior year by formula. The reconciliations show residual differences and their explanation (reclassifications, an account kept outside the asset register). Book value per balance sheet vs Spiegel ties after one reclassification. Disposals are analysed both at cost (AHK) and at residual book value (RBW).
- **quirks**: 7 pasted screenshots (journal extracts, a legal-commentary clipping) and 2 ink annotations anchored in the text sections. 13 merged ranges, all over **prose paragraphs** (e.g. A58:I60, A223:K224) or remark cells, never over numbers. Orange (#FFC000) full-width section banners ("Wesentliche Zugänge", "Wesentliche Abgänge", "Prüfung …"). One account name holds the text "#N/A" (a pasted lookup error). Anonymised entity placeholders ("firm 1/2") in the text. Column-width definitions repeat far beyond the data (out to column ~WVU). Rows 376–866 are empty but formatted. Mostly Calibri 11. No freeze panes, no filters, no comments.
- **size**: ~220 non-empty rows. ~23 lead-schedule accounts in 3 colour groups. 4 small sub-tables (2–7 rows each). ~10 reconciliation blocks.
