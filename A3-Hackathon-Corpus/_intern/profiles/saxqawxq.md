# saxqawxq.pdf — layout profile

- **doc_type**: other. It is an SAP balance sheet and P&L (Bilanz/GuV structure report, RFBILA00-like) with a prior-year comparison, p.1–17: Aktiva, Passiva, GuV, then a "Nicht zugeordnete Konten" block. It is not an asset document, but the fixed-asset accounts and Bilanzpositionen tie to rrtvr/zt.
- **format**: no text layer; the glyphs are vector outlines (not a raster scan, no noise or skew). A black redaction bar with an "X" runs across the top of every page.
- **pages**: 17. Page box 595 x 791 pt with /Rotate 90, so it shows as landscape. The content of p.1–5 and p.7–11 is turned a further 90° (sideways), and the redaction bar clips its first characters. p.6 and p.12–17 are upright.
- **one line**: monospace SAP list with columns Position | Text Bilanz/GuV-Position | Sum.Berper | Sum.Verper | Abs. Abw. | Rel. Abw. The 7-digit position code repeats on every line. Sum lines are underlined with "====". Negatives use a trailing minus.
